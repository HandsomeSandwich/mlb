A handoff = one manager drops a player and another adds him within {{ data['gap_days'] }} days. For each, we measure the player's real MLB production (per game) in the {{ data['window_days'] }} days before vs after the pickup. Δ is the change in per-game value: +positive means the player took off after the handoff (the adder benefited); −negative means he regressed. Recently-added players show “pending” until games accrue.
