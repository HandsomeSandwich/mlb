Each rating z-scores a player per category against the pool of regulars, then averages those z's{% if result and result['weighted'] %} weighted toward the categories your team is weakest in (so a player who fixes your hole rates higher for you){% endif %} — 50 = average regular, +15 per SD. Every player also carries a positional-scarcity premium (±5): a good C or SS is worth more than an equally-productive 1B because the player you'd replace him with is worse (replacement level at roster depth = teams × slots). Pitchers split into SP / RP / closer; closer premiums stay small because saves are already in the rating. The adjusted column nudges for luck (AVG vs xBA, ERA vs FIP), capped at ±6. Edge nets each side against an average roster spot (50), so a 2-for-1 isn't unfairly tilted by player count. Decision support, not gospel.
